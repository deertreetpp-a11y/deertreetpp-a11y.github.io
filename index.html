✦ (FAQ)

FAQ.

Total transparency. If you don't see what you're looking for, just reach out and let's clear things up.

What services do you provide? +

Brand identity, web design, front-end development, and motion design. I focus purely on digital experiences.

How long does a typical project take? +

A landing page usually ships in 1–2 weeks. Full brand + site projects take 4–6 weeks depending on scope.

Can I update the website myself after launch? +

Yes — every project ships with a clean structure and a short walkthrough so you can edit content without a developer.

How do payments work? +

50% deposit to book the slot, 50% on delivery. Monthly plans are billed at the start of each cycle and can be paused anytime.

Do you offer ongoing support? +

Every launch includes 30 days of free fixes. After that you can move to a monthly plan or book ad-hoc support.
